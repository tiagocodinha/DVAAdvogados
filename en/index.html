Duarte, Vieira e Associados, RL is a law firm founded in 2006, dedicated to providing high-calibre legal services in Portugal and in international contexts. We advise companies, investors and individuals, combining deep legal expertise with a close, strategic and results-driven approach.

About Us

Duarte, Vieira e Associados, RL is a law firm with two decades of experience providing legal counsel to both domestic and international companies and individuals across the main areas of Portuguese law.

With a particular focus on corporate law, DVA Advogados brings together a multidisciplinary team that delivers integrated legal advice, guiding clients from the initial structuring of their projects through to consolidation and growth in the Portuguese market.

The firm's practice is defined by a rigorous, pragmatic approach focused on value creation, grounded in a relationship of trust and proximity with its clients.

At DVA Advogados, we place a strong emphasis on maintaining a direct and personal relationship with our clients, ensured by the active involvement of the partners and the team in every matter.

This close engagement allows us to gain a thorough understanding of each client's objectives, challenges and specific circumstances, and to deliver legal solutions that are effective, well-structured and tailored to their particular situation.

Direct, ongoing access to senior partners and the wider team enables a deep understanding of client needs, and the ability to craft legal solutions that are both robust and carefully calibrated to the realities of each business.

Our work is guided by exacting standards of rigour, independence and technical excellence, with a consistent focus on efficiency and sound decision-making.

Services

Our firm has a proven track record in advising investors who wish to establish themselves in Portugal. A dedicated team of specialists in immigration, tax, real estate, succession and corporate law allows us to provide a personalised service tailored to each client.

Portugal offers exceptional tax and immigration conditions for foreign nationals wishing to settle in the country. The Non-Habitual Resident (NHR) regime and the Golden Visa programme are amongst the most sought-after schemes available.

We represent and assist clients of all sizes and across all sectors in incorporation, merger and acquisition transactions, judicial and extrajudicial corporate restructurings, corporate governance matters, commercial contracts and the negotiation of partnerships and joint ventures.

Our Banking and Financial Law department advises on the opening of bank accounts in Portugal for foreign individuals and companies, with the speed and efficiency that our clients' business interests demand.

Comprehensive advice on residence permits, nationality applications and visas.

Full assistance with property transactions and real estate investments.

Tax advisory and international tax planning.

International Mobility and Investment

DVA Advogados represents and assists clients who wish to reside or invest in Portugal, providing legal support in obtaining residence permits — including Golden Visas — and in structuring tax arrangements under the Non-Habitual Resident regime.

We accompany the entire process in an integrated manner, ensuring a clear, secure and efficient legal approach at every stage.
